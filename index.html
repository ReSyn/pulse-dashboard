SMALL HABITS THAT HELP

Hydration basics

01
Keep water nearby

Have a drink with meals and snacks, and sip regularly through the day.

02
Sports and hot weather

Drink before and after activity, and take water breaks during longer sessions or in the heat.

03
Listen to your body

Thirst is a useful cue. Needs vary with weather, activity and the person, so don't force a fixed amount.

04
Food helps too

Fruit, vegetables, soups and other foods also contribute to hydration.

Keep it balanced

Water is a great everyday choice. You don't need to track every sip, and sports drinks aren't usually necessary for ordinary daily activity. If you feel unwell or unusually thirsty often, talk with a parent or healthcare professional.
